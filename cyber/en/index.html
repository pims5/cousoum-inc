Our commitment

Trust requires clarity.

  • Written authorisation before any intervention
  • Read-only by default
  • Facts distinguished from statements
  • Limits and unverified items made explicit
  • Data limited to what is necessary and handled as agreed

Our limits

Clear and stated boundaries.

  • No 24/7 monitoring
  • No commercial penetration testing presented as available
  • No certification or guarantee of absolute security
  • No compliance claim without verification
